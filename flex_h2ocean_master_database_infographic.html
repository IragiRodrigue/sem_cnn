OER Descriptor & *OOH Stabilization

As demonstrated in Paper 1, introducing Ru into the Fe-Nb-O matrix regulates charge distribution. The engineered sites actively transfer charge to coordinated surface oxygen, vastly stabilizing intermediate *OOH adsorption on Ru and Fe centers.

HER Free-Energy (ΔGH*)

Calculations identify the Mn site as the optimal active center for hydrogen evolution (≈ 0.08 eV). Paper 2 reveals that high-valence Nb and Ta at the B-sites promote favorable d-band alignment, enhancing orbital overlap with oxygen p-orbitals to lower activation barriers.
